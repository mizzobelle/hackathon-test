    Where the human stays in control

    • Documentation only. AuditPilot judges whether the notes record something. It never judges whether the clinical care was right, and never suggests a treatment change.
    • Every judgement quotes its source. If the tool cannot quote a sentence from the notes, the answer is Not documented — never an assumption that it probably happened.
    • Conflicts stop the machine. Where the notes contradict themselves, the cell returns Needs review and refuses to pick a side.
    • The clinician signs off each row. No row counts towards the report until it is confirmed. The tool proposes; the human decides.
    • No storage, no integration. No database, no login, no localStorage. Refresh the page and it is gone.
    • Improvement actions change the record, not the care. Every suggested action is a form field, a system setting, a ward-round routine or a teaching point about recording. Nothing recommends a drug, a dose or a clinical threshold, and nothing is generated for an individual patient.

    AuditPilot is a documentation audit and service-evaluation tool. It reports whether information is present in a record and summarises that across a sample. It does not diagnose, does not assess or recommend treatment, produces nothing specific to an individual patient, and is not a medical device.
