    Scope and safety
    • Documentation only. Never a judgement on clinical care.
    • Every judgement quotes its source. No quote means Not documented, never an assumption.
    • Conflicts stop the machine. Contradictory notes return Needs review.
    • The clinician signs off each row before it reaches the report.
    • Actions change the record, not the care — form fields, system settings, ward routines. Never a drug, dose or threshold.
    • Nothing stored. No database, no login, no localStorage.

    AuditPilot is a documentation audit and service-evaluation tool. It does not diagnose, does not assess or recommend treatment, produces nothing specific to an individual patient, and is not a medical device.
